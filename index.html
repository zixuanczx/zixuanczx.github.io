I am a postdoc at TU Darmstadt and work with Prof. Carsten Binnig at the Systems group, before which I got my PhD degree and master degree at Northeastern University and Fudan University, advised by Prof. Mirek Riedewald and Prof. X.Sean Wang respectively.

My current research interest focuses on equipping databases with more weapons including optimizing, explaining and reasoning. In My PhD project, we studied how to use linear scoring functions to explain top-k tuples and rankings with optimization techniques. We also made our techniques practical by integrating them with a existing DBMS to equip it with the functionality of query refinement.
